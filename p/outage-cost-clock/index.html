Recent outages, in the providers' words

Square, Nov 4
Payment Acceptance and Point of Sale listed 2:27 PM to 3:52 PM. Square said the disruption was resolved and it had implemented measures to strengthen its systems. No cause stated.
Microsoft 365, Nov 12 to 14
A latent bug from July plus gateway load and over 14% of server capacity unavailable, per Microsoft's Charles Lamanna as reported. Restored by Saturday; a final report was promised.
Cloudflare, Oct 23
A connectivity-provider outage caused congestion and packet loss in North America. Resolved 18:43 UTC.
Azure networking, Sep 30 to Oct 1
Gateway updates resumed after a deployment restriction expired and overwhelmed a regional control plane, per Azure's review. About 5 hours 45 minutes.
