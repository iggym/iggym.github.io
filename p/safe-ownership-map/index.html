The mechanics, in plain words

  • A post-money SAFE's ownership, just before the priced round, is its amount divided by its post-money cap. YC's page gives $1M at 15% as a $6.7M cap.
  • Stacking SAFEs adds those slices up. They come out of everyone who held shares before, including the unissued pool.
  • Then the priced round adds new shares and, here, tops the pool up to your target. That dilutes everyone, SAFE holders included.
  • If the round price comes out below a SAFE's price, that SAFE's real terms differ from this model. Read the document.
