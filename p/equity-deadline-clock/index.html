What stays the same everywhere

  • The 83(b) election is due no later than 30 days after the date the property was transferred (Form 15620 instructions).
  • The person making the election must also give a copy to the company (Form 15620 instructions; Treas. Reg. 1.83-2(d)).
  • The IRS online filing route for Form 15620 uses an ID.me account, per law-firm summaries; read the IRS page before relying on it.
  • Your option agreement sets the exercise window; the ISO test in IRC 422(a)(2) looks at employment up to 3 months before exercise.
