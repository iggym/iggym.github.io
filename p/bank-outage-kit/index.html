What was reported

Chase, Nov 16 2026 (reported)
Errors reported from about 11:30 a.m. ET. Chase said a temporary issue related to a software bug in on-premise hardware systems, with no evidence it was cyber-related and not systemic, and that customers still had access to accounts and funds while some transactions were slow. A Chase spokesperson described restoration at about 6:30 p.m. ET. The restoration statement, as reported, did not detail compensation.
Revolut, Oct 20 2026 (company postmortem)
A power failure in one Google Cloud London zone at about 06:00 UTC. Card authorisations were back by 12:40 UTC, app access from 15:45 UTC, everything by 17:15 UTC. Revolut said funds and data stayed safe and that it deployed changes to process card reversals immediately after a payments backlog clears.
