How these are made

Same rules as the rest of this site: hand-written HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript, no build step and no framework. They work without JavaScript, follow your light or dark setting, keep no accounts, and don’t track you. Each one lives in its own repository, so the source link on any card takes you straight to the code.

If one of them is useful — or hard to read on your device — I’d like to hear about it. The contact links are on the homepage.
