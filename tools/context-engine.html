Portable Context Engine

Keep the instructions you give AI assistants over and over, like your role, your preferences, and house rules, as reusable profiles. Fill in the details that change, add today’s task, and copy one block you can paste into any AI chat.

Stays on your device Remembers your work

How to use it
  1. Pick a profile or add your own.
  2. Write your standing instructions. Put {{name}} wherever something changes from task to task. Edits save automatically.
  3. Fill in the boxes that appear for each {{name}}, then describe today’s task.
  4. Copy the result and paste it at the start of a new AI chat. Use Export to back up your profiles or move them to another browser.
