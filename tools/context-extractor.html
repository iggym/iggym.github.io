Context Extractor

Paste a long AI chat, meeting notes, or a project thread. This page pulls out the goals, rules, decisions, open questions, and key facts, and arranges them into a short summary. Review each item, fix anything it sorted wrongly, then copy a ready-made handoff prompt into a fresh chat so you don’t have to start over.

Stays on your device Remembers your work

How it works
  • Your text is split into sentences and lines, and each one is sorted by the words it uses. For example, “must”, “never”, and “only” point to a rule, and a line ending in “?” is an open question.
  • Repeats, greetings, and chit-chat like “thanks” or “sounds good” are dropped.
  • If the result is over your size limit, the least important sections are trimmed first. Within a section, the oldest items go first, because later messages usually replace earlier ones.
  • It’s a keyword sort, not an AI summary. Read the result before you rely on it.
