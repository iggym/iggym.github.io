Context Window Optimizer

A model can only read so much text at once. That limit is its context window. Paste a prompt to see roughly how much of the window your instructions and content take up, and how much room is left for the reply.

Stays on your device

How to use it
  1. Paste your prompt. To split it, put standing rules after [SYSTEM_PROMPT] and the material to work on after [USER_CONTENT]. Without markers, everything counts as content.
  2. Choose a window size close to the model you use, and how long a reply you expect.
  3. If the bar runs out of room, trim the content first. Rules are usually short and worth keeping.
