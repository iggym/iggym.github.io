Prompt Matrix Evaluator

Paste a prompt and get three things. A checklist of what it’s missing (task, output format, length, audience) that also flags vague words, contradictions, and leftover placeholders. One-click fixes for each gap. And two rewrites: a tightened copy with the filler removed, and a structured version with role, task, rules, and format laid out the way models follow most reliably.

Stays on your device Remembers your settings

How it works
  • The checks are plain rules, not an AI judge. They catch the gaps that most often produce vague or rambling answers.
  • Tightening only removes politeness and filler (“could you please”, “make sure to”, “thanks in advance”). Your actual request is never changed.
  • The structured rewrite keeps your words and adds bracketed placeholders, like [Who is this for?], wherever something is missing. Fill them in before you send it.
  • Add phrases your team overuses under Your own filler, and they’ll be removed too.

Token counts are estimates (about four characters per token).

Each button edits your prompt and re-scores it.
