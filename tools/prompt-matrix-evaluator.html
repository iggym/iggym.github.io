Prompt Matrix Evaluator

Paste a prompt to get two things. First, a checklist of what it’s missing: a clear task, an output format, a length limit, an audience. It also flags vague words and contradictions. Second, a tightened copy with the filler removed, plus what that saves in tokens and money at your request volume.

Stays on your device Remembers your settings

How it works
  • The checks are plain rules, not an AI judge. They catch the gaps that most often produce vague or rambling answers.
  • Tightening only removes politeness and filler (“could you please”, “make sure to”, “thanks in advance”). Your actual request is never changed.
  • Add phrases your team overuses under Your own filler, and they’ll be removed too.

Token counts are estimates (about four characters per token).
