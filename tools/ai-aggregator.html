Where your keys and questions go
  • Your question goes straight from this browser to OpenAI and Anthropic, and is billed to your own accounts. It never passes through my server.
  • Keys stay in this tab’s memory unless you tick “Remember keys on this device”. Only do that on a computer you trust, and use Forget keys to remove them.
  • For safety, use a key with a low spending limit. Anyone with access to this browser profile could read a remembered key.
