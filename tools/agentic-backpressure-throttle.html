Token Router

AI providers limit how many tokens you can use per minute. When a person is waiting on an answer and a big background job arrives at the same time, a router can serve the person first and hold the background job until the budget refills, instead of letting both slow down or fail.

Simulation

What this shows
  • The bar is a shared budget of 100,000 tokens that refills by 15,000 every tick, like a per-minute limit.
  • A live chat request (35,000 tokens) and a batch summary job (75,000 tokens) arrive together. That’s 110,000, more than the budget holds.
  • The router runs the urgent request now and makes the batch job wait until there’s enough room. Nothing fails.
  • This is a simulation. The numbers are illustrative.
